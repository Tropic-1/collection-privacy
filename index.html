Privacy policy

Completist (Android app com.tropicfields.collection) · Effective 25 November 2026

Completist helps you keep track of the things you collect. It is built so that your collection stays on your phone. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics. We (the developer) do not receive, see or store any of your collection data.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your collection: sets, items, values, prices paid, sellers, conditions, dates, locations, names of people you lent items to, notes you enter, and Hunt mode trips (where you were shopping and what you found and paid). This is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • Photos and receipts you take with your phone's camera app, choose from your phone, or download from a link. The app makes a resized copy and keeps it in the app's private storage. It does not read your photo library (you pick each photo with the system picker) and it does not ask for camera permission: taking a photo opens your phone's own camera app, which hands the one photo back to Completist.
  • Pro: whether you have Completist Pro is stored on your phone. Payment is handled by Google Play (see below).
  • Barcodes: when you scan a barcode, the app reads it from the photo on your phone and keeps only the number (with the item or as a search). The scan photo itself is not saved.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes all of this from your phone.

When the app connects to the internet

The app works without an internet connection. It only goes online when you ask it to, and Offline mode in Settings blocks every connection the app makes:

  • Picture from a link: when you paste a link to a picture or web page, the app downloads it directly from that website. Like any download, the website receives your IP address and a standard request.
  • Pictures and prices: when you search for a picture or a value, the app sends your search words (or a saved item number to refresh a price) directly from your phone to the service you picked, and downloads the picture or price you choose:
    • Book covers: Open Library (openlibrary.org)
    • Album covers: MusicBrainz and the Cover Art Archive (musicbrainz.org, coverartarchive.org)
    • Card pictures and prices: Scryfall (api.scryfall.com) and TCGdex (api.tcgdex.net)
    • Game details (title, year, platform, publisher; no pictures): Wikidata (www.wikidata.org)
    Scanning a book's ISBN barcode looks the book up on Open Library in the same way, and scanning a record's or CD's barcode looks it up on MusicBrainz. Checking a barcode in Hunt mode only searches your own sets on your phone. These services run under their own privacy policies.
  • Matches while you type (off unless you turn it on in Settings or in Add item): when you add an item to a set of books, games, records or CDs, or cards, the name you type is sent to the matching service above once you pause typing, and the app shows what it finds. Nothing is added unless you tap a result. The picture of a result you pick loads from that service when you view the item, until you save it to your phone. Only the name you type is sent, never your collection list or personal details.
  • Exchange rates: when you look up or update a price, the app fetches exchange rates from Frankfurter (api.frankfurter.dev) so prices appear in your currency. The request contains no details about you or your collection.
  • Daily value updates (off unless you turn them on in Settings): once a day when you open the app, it asks those services for current prices of the items you got an online price for, using their item numbers. None of this includes your collection list or any personal details.
  • Full checklists: when you create a set from online, the app sends the set, author or artist name you search for to the same services, and downloads the list. Pictures in these sets load from those services when you view them, and each service sees your IP address like any website, until you save the pictures to your phone.
  • Buying or restoring Pro: when you open the Pro screen, buy Pro or restore a purchase, the app connects to Google Play through Google's Play Billing library. If you have bought Pro, the app also checks with Google Play now and then when it opens, so a refund or a finished pending payment is noticed. The payment itself is handled entirely by Google Play under your Google account and Google's privacy policy; we never see your card or bank details. Like every paid app, we receive Google Play's order records for sales (such as order number, date, country and price). Google's billing library also sends Google technical logs about these billing requests (for example whether they succeeded and connection problems), with basic device details, which Google uses to run and improve Play Billing. If you never open the Pro screen, this library is not started. Offline mode blocks it.
  • Feedback and ratings: "Send feedback" in Settings opens your own email app with our address filled in, and "Rate on Google Play" opens the Play Store. The app sends nothing by itself.
  • Price links: the "eBay sold" and "PriceCharting" buttons open those websites in your browser with the item's name as the search. The app itself sends nothing; your browser and those websites' own policies apply.

All connections the app makes use encrypted HTTPS where the website supports it.

Backups

  • Backup files: "Back up" in the app saves a file containing your collection and photos to a place you choose (for example your phone's storage or a cloud drive app). You control that file.
  • Automatic backups (optional): if you choose a backup folder, the app saves a backup file there by itself every day or week and keeps the newest five. If that folder belongs to a cloud service (such as Google Drive or OneDrive), the files are stored by that service under your account and its privacy policy. You can turn this off at any time in Settings.
  • Android backup: if Android backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include the app's data in your personal Google backup, as it does for most apps. This is handled by Google under your Google account; we have no access to it. You can turn it off in your phone's settings.

Reports and app lock

  • Reports: an insurance report is made on your phone and only goes where you save, print or send it.
  • App lock (optional): uses your phone's own screen lock. The app never sees your fingerprint, face or PIN.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell or share personal data.
  • The app contains no advertising, and we add no analytics, crash-reporting or tracking code. The only third-party code in the app is Google's Play Billing library, used for Pro as described above.
  • The app asks for no permissions that need your approval. It declares internet access (for the actions above) and, for Pro, Google Play billing and network-status access.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
